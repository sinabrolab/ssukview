Made for reading, nothing else

📚

Open files as they are

ZIP and CBZ archives, PDFs and folders of JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC images open directly, with pages sorted naturally by file name.

☁️

Straight from the cloud

Read from Google Drive and Dropbox without downloading first — or save a book to the device for offline reading.

📖

Single or two-page

Spreads adapt to your screen and orientation. Keep the cover on its own, or read right-to-left for manga.

🔁

Continue anywhere

Sign in and your shelf, page and favorites follow you across devices. Your comic files are never uploaded.

🗂️

Series, grouped

Volumes from the same folder or title stack together, and the next volume is ready the moment you finish.

🔍

Magnifier and brightness

Check small lettering with the magnifier, and on phones slide up or down to set screen brightness.
