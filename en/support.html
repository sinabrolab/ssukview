FAQ

Which files can I open?

ZIP and CBZ archives, PDFs, and folders of JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP or HEIC images. Pages are sorted naturally by file name (1, 2, … 10).

How do I read books in the cloud?

In the Import (가져오기) tab, connect Google Drive or Dropbox and pick a folder. Pages are cached on the device only while you read (up to 500 MB); choose “Save offline” from a book’s ⋯ menu to read without internet.

How do I continue on another device?

Sign in with the same account on both devices; your shelf, page and favorites sync automatically. Files are never uploaded, so import the same file on the other device or open the cloud book — cloud books appear under “Books on other devices” in the Import tab.

I want to read right-to-left (manga)

While reading, tap the center of the screen, open the settings button at the top right and set the reading direction to right → left.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads (₩4,900, one time) from My Account (내 계정). It is not a subscription — you pay once. Ads only appear occasionally after you finish a book, never while reading. The Mac app has no ads.

How do I restore my purchase or get a refund?

On a new device or after reinstalling, tap My Account → Remove Ads → Restore purchase. With the same Apple or Google account you don’t pay again. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Installing on Mac

The DMG on this site is notarized by Apple and opens without warnings. Open it and drag SSUK View into Applications. If macOS warns you, the download may be damaged — please download it again.

Delete your account

In the app — open My Account (내 계정) → Delete account (회원 탈퇴) and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

If you can’t use the app — email admin@sinabrolab.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “SSUK View account deletion request”. We verify it’s you and delete the account within 10 days.

What is deleted — your account (email, nickname), all sync records (book titles, pages read, favorites, cloud file locations) and reader settings are deleted immediately and not retained. For Apple sign-in we also ask Apple to revoke our access.

What remains — books and reading history stored on your device stay there until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and Google as required by law; the Remove Ads purchase stays with your store account and can be restored.
